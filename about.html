About me

I take on small, straightforward painting jobs around the Limerick area.

Simple jobs done carefully

I focus on rooms and surfaces that are already in reasonably good condition. That usually means clearing and protecting the area, filling small holes or cracks, light sanding where needed, and painting carefully.

I keep the scope sensible

I am not trying to take on every kind of decorating job. If a room needs major plaster repair, damp treatment, specialist access or extensive remedial work, it is probably not the right job for me.

Easy first contact

The easiest way to start is to send me a WhatsApp message with your area, what you want painted and a few photographs. I can usually tell quickly whether it is the kind of job I take on.
