AS PAINTING · CAHERDAVIN, LIMERICK

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about arranging a painting project in Limerick.

How far are you prepared to travel?

Roughly 50–60 kilometres, or about one hour of driving, depending on the journey.

How do I get a painting quote?

WhatsApp on 086 814 0362 with the location, a short description and a few photographs—or simply call.

Will the agreed price change?

The scope and price should be agreed before work begins. Any additional work can be discussed and quoted separately before proceeding.

How long will I wait for a reply?

Text messages and voice messages are welcome on WhatsApp. Messages will be acknowledged as quickly as possible and calls returned when available.

How soon can you start?

Availability varies. Send the job details and preferred timing and we can discuss current availability.

Can I send photographs first?

Of course. Send the room, exterior or surface on WhatsApp and include a short description of the work you need.

Who supplies the paint?

This can be agreed before anything is bought. The correct product depends on the surface, preparation, priming and finish required.

What does high-quality work mean?

Personal service, careful preparation, correct priming and patient application. Quality remains long after cost is forgotten.

How long will the painting work take?

That depends on the size and condition of the job, especially the preparation required. A clearer estimate can be given once the work is understood.
