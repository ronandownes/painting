Painting technology

Technology can help you make a confident colour decision, while good tools and methods support the quality of the finished work.

See your room in different colours

Send a clear photograph of your room and tell us the colours or general style you prefer. We can prepare digitally edited options showing approximately how the room may look before painting begins.

A screen preview is a practical visual guide. Actual paint colour can vary with daylight, artificial lighting, surface texture and screen settings, so final samples still matter.

Preparation and priming

High-quality painting starts with surface preparation. Filling, sanding, cleaning, dust control and the correct primer can matter more to the final appearance than simply adding another coat of paint.

Application methods

Brushes, rollers and spray application each have useful roles. The method should suit the surface, access, product and finish rather than being used simply because it is fashionable.

Checks before painting

Moisture, loose material, stains, bare plaster, timber condition and previously painted surfaces can all change what preparation is needed. The correct system is decided by the condition of the job, not by one universal recipe.
